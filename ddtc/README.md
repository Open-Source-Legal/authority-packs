# `ddtc` — DDTC published practice — guidance, enforcement, CJ and advisory opinions

What the Directorate of Defense Trade Controls says and does, as distinct from what the ITAR provides. Five corpora because practitioners cite these separately, one pack because they are one publisher on one portal with no release cadence at all — the provenance story is identical for every section in it.

Carried below IMPLEMENTING weight throughout. After Loper Bright the legal status of agency guidance is more contestable, and the distinction should be structural rather than a caveat the model has to remember.
