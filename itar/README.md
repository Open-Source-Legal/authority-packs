# ITAR authority pack

**Harvested verbatim, not legally reviewed.** Every section body is verbatim
text pulled from the official sources below by the scripts in `../../harvest/`.
There are no stubs. No attorney has reviewed the result — that is what
`approval_status: harvested_unreviewed` in each charter records.

## Design notes

- **USML keys are lowercase Roman numerals** (`usml:viii`). Authority-key
  matching is case-sensitive on the section part and the extractor lowercases;
  an uppercase key is unreachable from any real citation. Verified against a
  live install: `usml:VIII` resolves to `None`, `usml:viii(a)` falls back to
  `usml:viii`.
- **`itar:121.1` is an index, not the USML.** § 121.1's body *is* the entire
  Munitions List, which this pack also carries as 21 addressable `usml:*`
  documents at the same weight. Carrying it whole in both places would let
  retrieval surface one ~200 KB blob instead of the precise category. It holds
  the verbatim heading, the verbatim `(a)-(b) [Reserved]` lead-in and the
  verbatim list of the 21 category headings, plus INCORPORATES relationships.
- **Part-level documents exist deliberately** so that "Part 122 of the ITAR"
  resolves. They are indexes assembled from the part's own verbatim headings,
  authority note and source credit.
- **No spec-level `aliases`.** They stamp every document in the corpus and
  override the namespace rows.
- **`rewrite_rules` are inert in a pack** — only `equivalences` are loaded.
- **`abbreviations` cannot carry federal entries** — only `state`/`municipal`
  tables exist. Surface strings live in namespace `aliases`.
- **AECA dual numbering** (§ 38 = 22 U.S.C. § 2778) is handled by equivalences;
  the U.S.C. number is canonical. All 54 pairings were read out of each
  section's USLM `sourceCredit` — the enacting "(Pub. L. 90-629, ch. N, § M,
  ...)" clause — rather than taken from a mapping table.
- **1,372 equivalence rows** are generated by `build/gen_equivalences.py --write`
  into a marked block; rows above the marker are hand-maintained and preserved.

### What has NOT been exercised

The four per-corpus personas (`personas/*.txt`) were revised for this build —
the USML asterisk/SME and "(MT)" rule, the ITAR "what silence means" rule, the
DDTC KB-number-and-dates rule — and **none of them has been run**. Both gold
passes queried the document corpus, so every answer went through that corpus's
`corpus_agent_instructions` (`../../corpus/persona.txt`); a per-corpus persona
applies when its authority corpus is queried directly, which nothing in either
run did.

They are four shipped prompt surfaces at CONTROLLING / IMPLEMENTING /
INTERPRETIVE weight with no test behind them. Treat them as unverified until a
gate exercises the authority corpora directly.

### Currency caveat carried in the pack

At the 2026-08-10 capture the eCFR flags one amendment as published but **not
yet incorporated into its own compiled text**: 91 FR 46281 (23 July 2026), USML
Category I firearm suppressors. Every USML section carries the capture date and
that notice in `metadata`; **Category I alone** carries a delimited currency
note in its body, because that is the only category the amendment touches.

This is normal in this field, not an anomaly — the eCFR is a compilation and the
Federal Register is operative on publication. Run
`python3 harvest/fedreg_watch.py --fail-on-stale` before relying on this pack.
