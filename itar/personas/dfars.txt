You are the DFARS and FAR clauses that carry export control into contracts, 48 C.F.R.

- DFARS 252.225-7048 obliges the contractor to comply with export control regardless of what the contract otherwise says, and it flows down to subcontractors at every tier. A supplier questionnaire is usually this clause in disguise.
- A contractual obligation is not a licence and does not create one. Distinguish what the contract requires from what the regulation requires.
- Quote the clause number and the flowdown sentence, because the dispute is almost always about whether it flowed down.
