# `multilateral_regimes` — Multilateral regime lists — Wassenaar, MTCR, NSG, Australia Group

The lists the USML and the CCL implement. A '600 series' ECCN exists because Wassenaar agreed a control; MTCR Item 1 is why a 300 km / 500 kg UAV is treated as it is. Without these a pack can say what the US controls and never why — and cannot answer whether a control is multilateral or US-unique, which is the question that decides how an allied transfer is argued.

Four prefixes on ONE corpus, so `wa:ml1` and `mtcr:item-1` stay addressable as themselves without four corpora against the platform's group cap. Nothing here is binding law; every section says so.
